Refund policy

Every Albumwright Pro payment comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee, including renewals.

Effective date: 24 October 2026

In short

  • Ask within 14 days of any Pro payment, first purchase or renewal, and we refund that payment in full. You don't need to give a reason.
  • Email support@albumwright.com from the email address of your Albumwright account.
  • We refund through Stripe, our payment provider, to the payment method you used.

Our 14-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy with Albumwright Pro, ask for a refund within 14 days of the day you were charged. The guarantee applies to your first purchase and to every renewal, monthly or yearly. You don't need to give a reason, and it doesn't matter whether you have started using Pro. We refund the full amount of that payment, including any tax that was collected with it.

Albumwright Free costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email support@albumwright.com, ideally from the email address of your Albumwright account.
  2. Tell us which payment you want back. The date and amount are on the receipt email for that payment. If you can't find it, tell us the email address of your account and roughly when you paid.
  3. We confirm your request and refund the payment through Stripe, at the latest within 14 days of your request. The money goes back to the payment method you used. Your bank may take a few more working days to show it.

What happens to Pro after a refund

When we refund a payment, we also cancel the subscription so it won't renew, and your account goes back to the Free plan. Your albums, songs and files stay on your computer, and you can keep using Albumwright's free features.

The guarantee is there for people who find Pro isn't right for them. Where repeated refund requests for the same or linked purchases are clearly an attempt to use Pro without paying, we may decline them. This never affects a refund you are entitled to by law.

Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time from Manage billing, in Albumwright's Settings → Account or on your account page at account.albumwright.com, or by emailing us. Cancelling stops future renewals, and Pro keeps working until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling on its own doesn't refund the current period: if you want your money back, ask within 14 days of the payment.

Your legal right to cancel (UK and EU consumers)

If you are a consumer in the UK or the European Union, the law usually gives you 14 days to cancel a contract made online, starting the day after the contract is made. For digital content and services that you ask to start straight away, that legal right can end, or you may have to pay for what you have used, once Pro starts. At checkout you may be asked to agree to this.

Our guarantee gives you more than the law requires: a full refund if you ask within 14 days of any payment, whether or not you have used Pro, and on renewals as well as your first purchase. This policy doesn't reduce your legal right to cancel.

To cancel, tell us clearly. You can use this wording in an email to support@albumwright.com:

“I give notice that I cancel my contract for Albumwright Pro. Order number: [ ]. Ordered on: [ ]. Name: [ ]. Email address used for the order: [ ].”

We will refund you within 14 days of receiving your notice, using the payment method you used.

If Albumwright Pro is faulty

If Albumwright Pro is faulty or not as described, you also have legal rights that last beyond 14 days, including under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 in the UK or the consumer law of the country where you live. These include a repair or replacement and, if we can't fix the problem within a reasonable time, a price reduction or refund. Please email us. Nothing in this policy affects those rights.

After 14 days

Outside the guarantee, payments are not refundable, except where the law requires a refund, or where we stop offering Albumwright before the end of a period you have paid for. In that case we refund the unused part.

Suno credits and AI plans

We can't refund Suno credits or subscriptions, or ChatGPT or Claude plans or usage, because you pay those companies directly. Please contact Suno, OpenAI or Anthropic about their own refund policies.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before asking your bank or card provider to reverse a payment. A refund under our guarantee is usually faster, and it keeps your order history clear.
