Privacy policy

How SynaptrixAI Ltd handles personal data for Albumwright. Albumwright is built so that your music, prompts, lyrics and logins never reach us.

In short

  • Albumwright runs on your computer. Your briefs, prompts, lyrics, music, library and AI conversations stay in your Chrome profile and your album folders, and travel directly between you and Suno, OpenAI or Anthropic.
  • We never receive your Suno account details or your ChatGPT or Claude logins.
  • If you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy takes your payment and shares your order details with us. Albumwright checks your licence key with Lemon Squeezy when you activate it and about once a week.
  • If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you.
  • If you create an Albumwright account, we keep your email address and when you signed in, on our own server in France. You can delete the account yourself at any time.
  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics. The account pages at account.albumwright.com set one strictly necessary cookie when you sign in.

What stays on your computer

Albumwright is designed so that your creative work never reaches us.

  • Albumwright stores your briefs, album plans, prompts, lyrics, singer choices, library and settings in your Chrome profile, and saves finished albums to folders on your computer.
  • To make music on Suno, Albumwright reads and operates the suno.com tab you are signed in to, inside your own browser. That page content is used on your computer and is not sent to us.
  • The Albumwright helper runs OpenAI's Codex program or Anthropic's Claude Code program on your computer, signed in with your own account. Your prompts and the AI's replies go directly between your computer and OpenAI or Anthropic.
  • Music generation and downloads happen directly between your browser and Suno.

We don't receive, store or have access to any of this. Suno, OpenAI and Anthropic process it as your own service providers, under their own terms and privacy policies, which we encourage you to read. We are not responsible for how those companies handle your data.

We also never receive your Suno password, your ChatGPT or Claude login, or the sign-in details those programs keep on your computer.

Personal data we process

Licence activation and checks (Pro only)

When you activate Pro, Albumwright sends your licence key and an anonymous device label to Lemon Squeezy's licence service, and repeats the check about once a week. The device label lets your key count computers against the limit of three; it does not contain your name or email address. As with any internet request, Lemon Squeezy also receives your device's IP address. Through Lemon Squeezy we can see which licence keys are active and on how many devices.

Why: to activate Pro, apply the three-computer limit and confirm your licence is valid. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR and EU GDPR).

Purchases

Lemon Squeezy is our online reseller and Merchant of Record. When you buy Pro, you buy it from Lemon Squeezy, which processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. Lemon Squeezy shares order details with us: your name, email address, country, order and subscription details (product, plan, price, dates, status and refunds), and your licence key and its activations. We never receive your full card number.

Why: to provide Pro, handle cancellations and refunds, answer questions about your order, prevent fraud and keep accounting records. Legal bases: performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)); compliance with our legal obligations for accounting and tax records (Article 6(1)(c)); and our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and in establishing or defending legal claims (Article 6(1)(f)).

We use your email address to contact you about your purchase and licence. We only send you marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Support and private beta emails

When you email support@albumwright.com, including to ask for a place in the private beta, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, your message and any attachments.

Why: to reply to you, fix problems, run the private beta and improve Albumwright. Legal bases: our legitimate interests in answering and supporting the people who use Albumwright (Article 6(1)(f)), and performance of our contract with you where your message is about a purchase (Article 6(1)(b)).

Please don't send passwords, sign-in codes or payment card details by email.

This website

albumwright.com is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics, advertising or tracking, and loads no fonts, scripts or images from other websites. When you visit, GitHub processes your IP address and request details to deliver the pages and may log them for security purposes, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We don't receive those logs.

Legal basis: our legitimate interests in running a secure website (Article 6(1)(f)).

The “Join the private beta” buttons open your own email program. Nothing is sent to us until you send that email.

Albumwright account (optional)

You can create an Albumwright account at account.albumwright.com by asking for a sign-in link. There is no password. When you do, we process your email address, the times you asked for a link and signed in, the name of the computer if Albumwright sends one, and a random session identifier for each place you are signed in (stored as a one-way hash). The email address of each link request is also recorded together with the IP address it came from, for 24 hours after the link expires, so we can stop abuse. The sign-in email is sent from hello@albumwright.com through Agentboxd, the email platform we operate ourselves.

These pages set one cookie, aw_session, only when you sign in. It is strictly necessary to keep you signed in and holds nothing but the session identifier. The server keeps request logs (IP address, page requested, outcome and time, never your email address or the link) for 14 days for security.

An account currently holds no music and no plan information: Albumwright keeps working on your computer whether or not you have one. You can sign out, see where you are signed in, and delete the account from the account page; deleting it removes your email address and every session at once.

Why: to sign you in without a password and to show you where you are signed in. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interests in keeping the sign-in service secure (Article 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and, where it applies to you, EU GDPR, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data;
  • object to our use of your data where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • receive data you gave us in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email support@albumwright.com. We will reply within one month. For complex requests we may extend this by up to two further months, and we will tell you why. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. There is normally no charge.

Some rights have legal limits. For example, we must keep order records for the periods the law requires.

Because your creative work never reaches us, requests about it should go to Suno, OpenAI or Anthropic. You can delete the data Albumwright keeps on your computer by removing the extension from Chrome, uninstalling the helper and deleting your album folders. Removing the extension deletes its library, so export anything you want to keep first.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113, or by post to Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF. If you live in the European Economic Area, you can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Children

Albumwright is not directed at children under 16, and you must be 18 or over to buy Pro. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

Security

We protect the personal data we hold with appropriate technical and organisational measures, use reputable providers, and limit access to people who need it to do their work. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify you and the regulator where the law requires.

Automated decisions

We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. The weekly licence check only confirms whether a licence key is valid.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date. If a change significantly affects how we use your personal data, we will tell Pro customers by email before it takes effect.
