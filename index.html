How it works

Four steps, from brief to album folder.

Albumwright sits in Chrome's side panel next to your signed-in Suno tab. You make the creative calls; it does the planning, writing, sending and packaging.

  1. BriefYour idea becomes a plan and a credit estimate.
  2. SingerAuditions become one named private Suno Voice.
  3. TracksLyrics and style prompts go to Suno, one by one.
  4. DeliverDownloads and covers become the album folder.

Step 1 of 4

Brief

Describe the record you want, in your own words.

You decide
The concept, genre and mood, the mix of vocal and instrumental tracks, and how many tracks.
Albumwright plans
The artist identity, the tracklist, and a mood and energy arc for every track.
A Suno credit estimate, shown before anything is spent.

Step 2 of 4

Singer

Audition voices before the album uses one.

You decide
How many candidates to hear, from 2 to 6, and Any, Female or Male for each one.
The winner. Pick by ear, or let the AI choose from your brief.
Albumwright runs
The auditions. Every candidate sings the same lyrics over the same backing, so only the voice differs.
The setup. Your chosen sample becomes a named private Suno Voice for the album's vocal tracks.

Step 3 of 4

Tracks

Every track is written and sent while the next one is being written.

You decide
When it runs. Pause and resume any time.
Each track's performance, vocal or instrumental, until it's sent.
Albumwright writes
A style prompt and original lyrics for each track, then sends it to Suno as soon as it's written.
It keeps a record of what it sent, so it never sends a track twice.

Step 4 of 4

Deliver

The finished album, downloaded and packaged in one folder.

You decide
MP3 or WAV.
Which version of each track goes into your playlists.
Albumwright packages
Every track, downloaded through Suno's own Studio export.
Lyrics, playlists, a tracklist, and front and back covers.

If a download is interrupted, it continues by itself when you reopen the panel.

What you get

An album folder you can release from.

  • Every track in MP3 or WAV, both versions Suno made.
  • A lyrics file for each track.
  • A playlist for each version, to play the album in order.
  • A tracklist.
  • Front and back covers, painted by ChatGPT's image generation or set as a typographic cover designed by Claude.

FAQ

Questions before you start.

Anything else? Email support@albumwright.com.

Do I need a paid Suno plan?

Yes: Suno Pro or Premier. Suno only allows downloads and commercial use on paid plans. Albumwright downloads through Suno Studio's own export, so your plan's download rules apply.

Who pays for Suno credits and AI usage?

You do, through your own plans with Suno and with OpenAI or Anthropic. We never charge for them. Before anything is spent, the Brief step shows a Suno credit estimate for the whole album.

Which AI does Albumwright use?

The one you link: your ChatGPT plan through OpenAI's official Codex program, or your Claude Pro or Max plan through Anthropic's official Claude Code program. You sign in on that company's own page, and usage counts against your plan.

Can Albumwright see my music, lyrics or logins?

No. Your briefs, prompts, lyrics and library are stored in your Chrome profile and your album folders. They travel directly between your computer and Suno, OpenAI or Anthropic. We never see your passwords or AI logins. The privacy policy lists the little we do receive.

Who owns the songs I make?

Albumwright claims no rights in what you make. Ownership and commercial use of the music, lyrics and artwork are governed by Suno's terms, and by OpenAI's or Anthropic's terms for the AI you use. Check them, and your plan, before you release anything.

Does it work on a Mac?

No. The Albumwright helper runs on Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), with Google Chrome.

What if I need to stop halfway through an album?

Pause and resume whenever you like; Albumwright never sends a track twice. Keep Chrome and the side panel open while an album runs. If downloads are interrupted, they continue by themselves when you reopen the panel.

How many computers can I use Pro on?

Each Pro licence key works on up to 3 computers.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of any Pro payment, including a renewal, and we refund it in full. The refund policy explains how.

What happens if Suno changes its website?

Albumwright works inside Suno's website in your tab, so a change there can stop a feature working until we update it. When a step can't be completed safely, Albumwright pauses and tells you why instead of guessing. We make reasonable efforts to keep up with changes, but we can't guarantee compatibility.

Is Albumwright made by Suno, OpenAI or Anthropic?

No. Albumwright is an independent product of SynaptrixAI Ltd. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Suno, OpenAI or Anthropic.
